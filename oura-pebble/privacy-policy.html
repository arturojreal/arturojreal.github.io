Privacy Policy

Pebble Oura Stats is designed to minimize how much data is stored and to give you control over access. Your health data is sensitive — here's exactly how it's handled.

App: Pebble Oura Stats Platform: Pebble / Rebble Last updated: December 12, 2025

📋1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Pebble Oura Stats ("Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Application with your Oura Ring and Pebble smartwatch.

By installing or using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Application.

🗃️2. Information We Collect

Information from Oura

When you connect the Application to your Oura account, Oura's API may provide:

  • Sleep information such as duration, timing, and quality metrics.
  • Activity metrics such as activity level and movement.
  • Readiness-related scores that Oura calculates.
  • Heart-related metrics like resting heart rate and heart rate variability, when available.
  • Other related metrics that Oura's API may make available for display.

Note: Your Oura password is never handled or stored by the Application. Authentication uses Oura's OAuth 2.0 flow, which is managed by Oura's own systems.

Device Information

The Application may access basic information about your device to function correctly, such as device type (Pebble smartwatch and paired phone, if applicable), operating system and version, and technical identifiers used by Pebble/Rebble as needed for communication.

Usage Information

To understand how the Application is used and to improve stability, we may process which features are used, frequency of use, and error and crash information. This information is used primarily to fix bugs, improve performance, and maintain the Application.

Information You Provide Directly

If you contact the developer (for example through GitHub issues or a website contact form), you may provide contact details, a description of an issue or feedback, or other information you voluntarily include in your message.

⚙️3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for:

  • Displaying Oura metrics on your Pebble device.
  • Maintaining, improving, and troubleshooting the Application.
  • Responding to support requests or feedback.
  • Meeting legal obligations, where applicable.

Information from Oura is used only to show your data within the Application and to keep values up to date.

💾4. Data Storage and Retention

Local Storage: Pebble Oura Stats primarily operates on your device. Health data from Oura is retrieved and then displayed or cached locally as needed for functionality and performance. There is no separate, centralized database maintained solely for storing user health data.

Access Tokens and Caching: To communicate with Oura's API, the Application may store an OAuth access token and limited state data. This information is kept only as long as necessary to maintain the authorized connection and provide the service.

Retention Period: Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. When you uninstall the Application or revoke access in your Oura account, any locally cached data and tokens related to the Application are removed or rendered unusable.

🔒5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information, including:

  • Use of Oura's OAuth 2.0 authentication, so passwords are never handled by the Application.
  • Communication with Oura's API over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Limiting stored data to what is necessary for functionality.

However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure. You use the Application at your own risk and are encouraged to use device-level security where available.

🔗6. Third-Party Services

Oura: The Application relies on Oura's API to access and display your metrics. Oura is responsible for its own data practices, governed by Oura's Privacy Policy and Terms, which you can review on their official website.

Pebble / Rebble: The Application runs on the Pebble/Rebble ecosystem, which may process limited device and usage data necessary for app distribution and operation. These platforms have their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing with Third Parties: The Application does not sell or rent your health information. Information may be shared only when required by law, when necessary to enforce Terms of Service or protect rights or safety, or with service providers who assist with hosting or technical operations under confidentiality obligations, if such providers are used.

✋7. Your Choices and Rights

Revoking Access: You can revoke the Application's access to your Oura data at any time by managing connected apps in your Oura account settings or by uninstalling the Application from your Pebble device. Once revoked, the Application cannot retrieve new data from Oura.

Data Deletion: Uninstalling the Application removes local data and tokens associated with it on your device. Because the Application does not maintain a separate persistent cloud database of your health data, there is typically no additional deletion step required.

Rights Under Certain Laws: Depending on your location, you may have rights under data protection laws (for example, GDPR in the EU or CCPA in California) such as the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, the right to object to or restrict processing, or the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. To exercise such rights, contact the developer via the channels below.

If it becomes known that information from a child has been collected inadvertently, reasonable steps will be taken to delete it and disable further use.

By using the Application, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred and processed outside of your country, and that different data protection laws may apply.

📝11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top shows the most recent revision. Continued use of the Application after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

🏥12. Important Health Data Note

Health and activity metrics are sensitive. The Application is designed to minimize how much data is stored and to give you control over access. The Application does not create or interpret medical diagnoses. For any concerns about the meaning of your health data, consult a medical professional.
