Oura <> Pebble

Display Your Health Metrics on Your Wrist

Pebble Oura Stats is a watchface application that brings Oura Ring health insights directly to a Pebble smartwatch. It shows sleep, activity, readiness, and heart-related metrics so users can quickly check key information without opening the Oura app.

How it works

01

Install

Install the watchface from the Pebble/Rebble app store onto your Pebble device.

02

Authorize

Authorize the app to access Oura data using Oura's secure OAuth login flow. Your credentials are never seen or stored by the app.

03

Display

Once connected, the watchface retrieves and displays your Oura metrics directly on your Pebble.

04

Stay in sync

The watchface periodically updates to keep information in sync with your latest Oura data throughout the day.

Data privacy & security

Your Oura data is sensitive and is treated accordingly. Authentication uses Oura's OAuth 2.0 flow, and your Oura password is never seen or stored by Pebble Oura Stats.

Health metrics are retrieved from the Oura API using secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and data is used only to display information on your Pebble. No unnecessary centralized storage of health data is performed beyond what is needed to provide the service.

For full details on how data is handled and what your rights are, please review the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
